# Binder Art Studio ✨ (beta)

Turn one favourite trading card into a full 9-pocket binder page. A free, step-by-step hobby tool made for the collecting community.

**Use it here:** https://bflutz.github.io/binder-art-studio/

## How it works
1. Pick a card and upload its picture.
2. Place it on a 3×3 binder page.
3. Download the template and the ready-made prompt.
4. Let ChatGPT paint the rest of the page around your card.
5. Upscale the result so it prints sharply.
6. Print it on A4 with cut marks.
7. Cut it out and fill your binder.

Everything runs in your browser. Your pictures are never uploaded anywhere by this site.
